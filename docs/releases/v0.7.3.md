# v0.7.3 — Texture streaming and Steam Deck input

Smoother HD textures: the packs no longer stall the render thread when they load, and the
fight frame rate with the packs on is back near the packs-off rate on Windows. The Steam Deck
no longer sees each menu press twice.

## Changes

- **HD textures stream in the background.** Replacement PNGs are decoded and mipped on up to
  three low-priority worker threads, on Windows, Linux and macOS. The render thread only creates
  the texture and copies the finished mip chain in; until a replacement is ready the original
  texture is drawn. `RECOMP_TEXTURE_SYNC=1` restores the old synchronous path. On the Steam Deck
  this cut the render-thread texture time from title to main menu from 4.9 s to 0.56 s, and the
  slowest single upload from 348 ms to 24 ms (scripted, packs on).
- **Texture identity on Windows.** The game identifies each bound texture by a SHA-256 of its
  bytes. Windows read the clock in 15.6 ms steps, so the 8 ms re-check window expired on nearly
  every bind and each texture was re-read every frame. The clock is now monotonic and
  sub-millisecond, and each texture's re-check backs off: 8 ms while its bytes move, up to 64 ms
  once stable. A texture whose bytes change on three checks in a row is drawn with its original
  texture for up to 500 ms between checks.
- **Texture cache 2 GiB on every platform** (was 512 MiB off Linux), so a four-fighter match keeps
  its replacements resident. Set `[textures] cache_mb` to change it.
- **Presentation pacing and colour upload** on Linux and macOS: the frame pacer sleeps to within
  0.3 ms of the deadline instead of spinning, and linear A8R8G8B8 textures convert straight into
  the upload buffer.
- **Steam Deck double input (D100).** Under Steam Input the physical Valve pad and Steam's
  virtual gamepad both reported each press. A physical Valve pad is ignored while a Steam virtual
  pad exists, at start-up and on hot-plug. A Steam pad moves up to the lowest free player when
  its player leaves; other controllers keep the player they were given.
- **Logging.** `RECOMP_TEXTURE_TRACE=1` logs `[TEXPACK]` every two seconds: replacements uploaded,
  source revalidations and full hashes. `[VKPERF]` lines show frame cost on the Vulkan renderer (Linux and
  macOS), and `[XA2]` lines show when the sound device runs dry.

## Measured

Windows, Release build, `faithful-hd` pack, one scripted fight (`regress.py --only fight`),
presents per 2 seconds (floor 110):

| Build | Traced | Untraced |
|---|---|---|
| Before this release (per-bind identity check) | 94 (failed) | 98 (failed) |
| v0.7.3 | 120, minimum 95 | 120, minimum 106 |

Packs off, the quick regression (unit tests, three golden frames, one fight) passes 5 of 5.
The Steam Deck figures are scripted measurements on the Deck, recorded in PR #20 (not a play-test).

## Known limits

- The Story tutorial fight on the Steam Deck averaged about 45 fps and was not reproduced by
  script; FFA holds 58 to 60 fps. The new `[TEXPACK]` and `[VKPERF]` lines are there to show where
  those frames go.
- Audio gaps were not verified by ear.
- The Windows numbers above are from the scripted harness; no owner play-test on Windows has been
  recorded for this release.
- The Steam move-up for a Steam pad was not run on a Steam Deck with Steam Input; the automated
  input fixture covers the other controllers.
- The identity cache still costs a SHA-256 for each texture whose bytes change. The next step
  would be write-tracking on guest memory, which is not in this release.
