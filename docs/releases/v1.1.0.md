# v1.1.0 — Your own music

Your own songs play like the game's soundtrack, a song cut from the original release is back, and two widescreen
glitches are fixed.

As always, you need your own supported USA Xbox dump. No game files or game executables are distributed.

## Downloads

- **Windows:** `DefJamRecompiled-1.1.0-windows-x64.exe`.
- **Linux and Steam Deck:** `DefJamRecompiled-1.1.0-linux-x86_64.AppImage`.
- **macOS (Apple Silicon):** `DefJamRecompiled-1.1.0-macos-arm64.zip`.
- The Windows and macOS setup tools (`DefJamSetup-1.1.0-windows-x64.exe`, `DefJamSetup-1.1.0-macos-arm64.dmg`) are
  attached as well. Every file has its SHA-256 and provenance.

## What's new

- **Your own songs (#203).** Put MP3, FLAC, Ogg or WAV files in the `songs` folder of your data folder (or add
  folders on the launcher's new Songs page). They are converted once and play in the Beatbox, in the menus with the
  song banner, and in fights, from start to end: when the game jumps around its music in a fight (the start, Blazin,
  knock-downs), your song carries on where it was. Up to 27 of your songs next to the game's own; 35 on at once.
  Each song plays **Straight** (as it is) or **Interactive** (experimental: the game switches it between filtered
  versions as the fight goes). Guide: [Custom songs](../custom-songs.md).
- **"Holler If Ya Hear Me" (2Pac) is back (#203).** Cut from the original release, its audio is still on the disc.
  It is on by default and appears in the Beatbox and the song banner like any other song.
- **Songs page in the launcher (#203).** Songs on the home screen: your folders, which songs are in the game and how
  they play, and each profile's Beatbox (every song on or off, and the order) with the mouse.
- **Next song / song from the start (#203).** Hold the right stick in (R3) and press D-pad right or left, or press F8
  or F7, in the menus and in fights. The D-pad press also reaches the game (in a menu it moves the cursor one step).
- **Volumes are kept (#203).** The music, effects and announcer volumes you set in Options now come back after a
  restart. The original game saved them but never applied them.
- **French-base translation packs (#185).** A language pack can now be built on the game's French text
  (`"base": "french"`) as well as the English one.

## Fixed

- **Widescreen: the song banner is no longer cut off (#204)** on menus without the HD Widescreen Menus mod.
- **Widescreen: the submission meter sits with its fighter's HUD (#205)**, for all four fighters, instead of a side
  bar's width away from it.

## For builders and testers

- Setup writes how long each stage takes to its log (#192).
- An optional compiler cache for setup builds (#195), off by default; setup does not turn it on yet.
- The memory check in the regression compares settled fights and reports scene changes separately (#201).

## Moving to 1.1.0

The app offers this release as an update. Then it offers **Update game**, which builds the game again from your dump.
Your saves, settings and mods are kept. Saves made with 1.1.0 stay readable by earlier versions and the original game:
your own songs' places in the Beatbox are kept in a separate file.
