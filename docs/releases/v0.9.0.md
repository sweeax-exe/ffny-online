# v0.9.0 — Widescreen (beta)

The game can now be played in widescreen. Pick a widescreen **Picture shape** on the launcher's
Display page and the 3D view gets wider (more of the arena and crowd at the sides, the same
vertical view), while menus, the fight HUD and movies keep their shape. It is off by default and
marked **beta**: this release is for play-testing it, on Windows first and now on Linux, the Steam
Deck and macOS.

## How to try it

1. Start the game; on the launcher's **Display** page set **Picture shape** to
   `widescreen (auto)` (your screen's shape: 16:9 or 16:10), or `16:9` / `16:10`. `21:9` and
   `32:9` are there too, marked experimental.
2. Under it, optionally: **Fight HUD position** (screen edges, or a centred 16:9 box on very wide
   screens), **Story cutscenes** (`authored` keeps the game's own 16:9 framing, `fill` always fills
   the screen), **Beside 4:3 pictures** (`blur` or `black`) and **Widest field of view**.
3. Press **Play**. Widescreen starts with the game: changing it later in the F1 overlay needs a
   restart. `4:3` and `stretch` work as before.

## What changes in widescreen

- **3D (fights, Free For All, Story hub, shops, cutscenes):** Hor+, a wider horizontal view at the
  same vertical view. The fight camera behaves as before; it just sees more at the sides.
- **Story cutscenes:** the game already framed them for 16:9 (a 640x360 letterbox at 4:3); in
  widescreen they fill a 16:9 screen with that framing, and get thin bars at 16:10.
- **Menus, loading posters and movies:** centred at their 4:3 shape, clipped to their box.
  On Windows, movies, posters and 2D-only menus get a soft blurred extension at the sides
  (`black` turns it off); on Linux and macOS the sides are black for now.
- **Fight HUD:** health bars, names and scores move to the screen corners (all four in a
  three- or four-fighter match); the timer and centred text stay in the middle.
- **3D inside menus** (the sketch artist's head and body, a created fighter's portrait on the
  versus poster, character previews) sits inside its panel.

## How it works

The game's own 3D view is widened in game code (`src/patches/widescreen.c`: all 3D aspect comes
from one function, and the game's field of view is horizontal). The renderer draws into a wider
canvas, places each 2D batch (centred, anchored to an edge during fights, or stretched for
full-screen fades and tints), and fills the sides beside centred pictures (toolkit fork branch
`defjam/widescreen`). The research, the design and the full work-package list are in
[docs/widescreen](../widescreen/README.md).

## Validation and limits

- **Windows (D3D11):** the owner's play-test at 16:9 (fights, Story mode with a created fighter,
  cutscenes, crib, shops, menus) and fixes from it: blurred sides over 3D scenes, torn tutorial
  prompts, `auto` taking the window's shape, the widescreen choices missing from the launcher, the
  sketch artist's model and the created fighter's poster portrait. Scripted checks at 16:9 and
  16:10 (one-on-one, Free For All, Story intro and cutscenes, crib, gym, Unlock Fighters, the
  character creator, a Story fight with a created fighter); boot soak 10/10 at 16:9. With
  widescreen off the quick regression is unchanged (golden frames identical).
- **Steam Deck (Linux, Vulkan):** scripted runs on a Steam Deck (2026-10-12, on main with v0.7.3's
  performance fixes and the HD packs with a 2 GiB cache): a one-on-one fight, a four-fighter Free For All
  and a four-minute Terrordome match at 16:10, the Story intro at 16:9 and 16:10, and a fight with the
  launcher setting `widescreen (auto)` in full screen (it resolves to 16:10, swap chain 1280x800). No
  crashes, saves restored, a median of 60 frames per second (120 presents per 2 s) everywhere, GPU time
  about 3 ms of the 16.7 ms frame. With widescreen off the loading-screen, title and main-menu golden
  frames match. Not covered: pause and popups, shops, the Story hub and the rest of the hand
  play-test list. **macOS (Vulkan/MoltenVK): see the Mac validation below.** The blurred side fill is
  Windows-only so far (black sides on Vulkan).
- **Known:** a one-pixel sliver of a slide-in element can show at the edge of the 4:3 box on two
  menus; 21:9 and 32:9 have not had a venue sweep (some arenas may show unfinished edges); Story
  cutscenes on screens wider than 16:9 widen instead of sitting in a 16:9 box; the pause menu and
  in-fight popups were not reached by the scripted checks.
- **Not in this release:** AI-extended movie sides (a to-do after the trial, see
  [08-fmv-ai-pipeline.md](../widescreen/08-fmv-ai-pipeline.md)); changing the shape without a
  restart.

## macOS validation

Installed the exact CI setup on Apple M1 Pro using only its bundled build
dependencies, then built the Mac correction with those same dependencies.
Scripted checks cover original/HD textures at 4:3 and 16:9, a 16:10 replay,
four-fighter HUD placement, Story intros at 16:9 and 16:10, and HD crib/gym
previews. All five capture variants match the 80-record Windows gameplay golden.

`widescreen (auto)` now accounts for a Mac screen's notch safe area, so a
notched 16:10 screen selects the fullscreen content shape. Explicit picture
shapes retain their behavior. The complete source suite passes 352 tests;
the full regression covers fights, combat/results, replay, Story intro/gym and
three successful soak boots. Corrected automatic fullscreen reaches combat at
16:10, and the launcher/F1 overlay render and close correctly. The raw full
regression retains known diagnostics; see the report below.

The strict audio underrun check also fails in the accepted pre-widescreen
Mac build (existing issue #11). The historical stream callback diagnostic appeared during an HD capture
and one off-mode crib boot; the symptom predates widescreen, and a subsequent
16:10 replay was clean. The crib was reached despite the diagnostic; its strict
failure is retained. Raw failures are retained. See the
[Mac validation report](../widescreen/09-macos-validation.md) for identities,
measurements, coverage and limits. The existing v0.8.0 setup-updater release
remains unchanged.
