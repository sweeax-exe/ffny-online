## Findings

Four candidate compositions have local textured geometry covering the full 640×480 APT stage: choosGF2 uses shapes 17 and 31 side by side; choosGF4, choosPart and charDec each use shape 3. Their mapped source IDs are absent from the reviewed 136, and the selected sheets are fully opaque. These are definitely outside the current custom-art review ledger; active root-frame use and any parent transforms remain unverified.

cribOptions shape 9 covers local bounds `[0,0,571,480]`: image 8 is fully opaque and image 7 is a translucent companion tail. A separate image 4 shape (bounds `[0,0,171,487]`) is an exact source-ID duplicate of the reviewed Options wall image 4. That reviewed identity is already covered; the cribOptions root placement/transform was not resolved.

The selected tournament sheets (names 2, 5 and 8, the 256×256 sources) belong to three paired shapes with local bounds no larger than 352×256. Their alpha is mixed, and none is a verified full-stage background in the decoded geometry. Treat them as bounded desk/paper candidates rather than widescreen-stage gaps. This does not claim that every tournament background or every menu background has been inventoried.

All six APT movies report a 640×480 stage. The table gives local APT shape bounds, not an assertion that the shape is placed at stage origin or used in the active screen. Full vertex XYZ and UV coordinates, material matrices, APT/object/XSH hashes, source aliases, and alpha pixel counts are preserved in JSON. Alpha bbox is `(left, top, right, bottom)` in native source pixels; counts list fully transparent, partial, and opaque texels.

## Limits

This report identifies candidate source groups and authored shape geometry. It does not resolve root timeline placements, active frames, parent transforms, clipping, motion, or runtime texture use. A full-stage local bounding box is strong geometric evidence for a stage background, but is not a play-state observation. The tournament selection is limited to its largest square source sheets, names 2/5/8, and is not a complete visual or semantic inventory.
