    What you get

    The fighter you remember. The online it never had.

    01

    Online in the main menu

    Choose ONLINE on the game's own main menu. Quick Match drops you straight into a fight; or browse the open rooms, open your own, or join a friend by code.

    02

    Rooms and venue vote

    One on One, 3-Way and Free for All with up to four players, CPU fighters in the free places. Pick from the roster, ready up, then the room votes on the venue.

    03

    King of the Hill

    Up to ten players in one room. Two fight, the rest watch and wait in line. The winner stays on; the loser goes to the back.

    04

    Voice chat

    Talk in rooms and fights: push-to-talk on the keyboard or the controller, an open microphone, or off, with a volume for every player.

    05

    Chat, friends and quick chat

    Five chat tabs, a controller keyboard, a quick-chat wheel, friends you can join, private messages, block, report and mute.

    06

    Crews and Crew Battles

    Make a crew, challenge another to 3v3 or 5v5 winner-stays-on, and climb the crew ladder from Street to City.

    07

    Ranked preseason

    Classic, Open and Draft, eight rank tiers from Street Rookie to Def Jam Legend, season rewards and a Locker.

    08

    Rollback netcode

    Rollback in every mode, with an automatic fallback when a connection cannot keep up.

    09

    Sharper than ever

    Up to four times the console's resolution, windowed or borderless, and optional HD textures generated on your PC from your own dump.

    10

    Any pad you own

    Xbox, DualSense, Switch Pro and most other gamepads, keyboard and mouse, rumble and remapping, up to four players on one PC.

    11

    Stats and leaderboard

    Sign in with Discord for your record, main fighter, last fights and the leaderboard.

    12

    Servers near you

    Relay servers in Europe and North America, with your ping shown in the game.

    Social Update

    Chat, friends and quick lines, in the game.

    Talk to the people you fight, from the game's own online screens, with a controller or a keyboard. Live since build 50.

    • Chat tabs: ROOM, GLOBAL, FRIENDS, MESSAGES and CREW, with Discord profile pictures.
    • A PlayStation-style on-screen keyboard for controllers.
    • A quick-chat wheel on L3 with lines you can edit.
    • Friends with online status: see who is on, join a friend's room.
    • Private messages, with a setting for who may write to you.
    • Block, report and mute from the player menu.
    • Chat during fights, for spectators.

    Crews and Crew Battles

    Your crew. Your name on the ladder.

    Start a crew with friends, then take on other crews. Crews arrived with build 51.

    • Create a crew with a name and a tag; roles Boss and Lieutenant; a private crew chat.
    • Challenge another crew to a 3v3 or 5v5, winner stays on.
    • A crew rating, leagues Street, Block, Borough and City, and a crew ladder.
    • Crew XP and levels with rewards such as a gold tag and a crown emblem.

    Ranked

    Climb from Street Rookie to Def Jam Legend.

    Ranked starts with a 9-day PRESEASON test week (build 52): play it, tell us what feels off, and the ladder resets before the real season.

    • Three modes: Classic, Open and Draft.
    • 8 rank tiers with new emblems, from Street Rookie to Def Jam Legend, and a promotion screen.
    • Season rewards: name colour, avatar frame, title and a Discord role.
    • The Locker is where you equip what you have earned.
    • Rollback netcode in every mode, with an automatic fallback when a connection cannot hold it.
    • See who is on top: the ranked ladder.

    The original Xbox game is translated into C and compiled on your own machine, so menus, Story mode and fights run natively at 60 frames per second, with sound, drawn through Direct3D 11.

    Get started

    Into the fight in three steps.

    1. 1

      Download the installer

      Get the Windows installer above. It is free and holds no game: only the port's code and the tools to build it.

    2. 2

      Point it at your dump

      Choose your own dump of the USA Xbox version, a disc image or an extracted folder. Setup checks it first. How to dump your disc

    3. 3

      It builds on your PC

      Setup translates and compiles the game locally, then the launcher opens. Press PLAY, choose ONLINE. Updates arrive through the launcher, signed.

    No game files are distributed, ever. PS2 and GameCube copies cannot be used. Online play is Windows only for now.
