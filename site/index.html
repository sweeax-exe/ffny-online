What you get

Everything you remember. Nothing holding it back.

  • 01

    Native on three platforms

    Windows x64 on Direct3D 11. Apple Silicon Macs and x86-64 Linux on Vulkan. Steam Deck runs the Linux build natively.

  • 02

    60 fps, up to 4× resolution

    Targets 60 frames per second with 1×–4× internal resolution. We're optimizing for low-powered hardware, and Steam Deck hits a steady 60 fps at 2×.

  • 03

    Widescreen, up to 32:9

    16:9, 16:10 and ultrawide. The HD Widescreen Menus mod redraws every menu and background to match, and original 4:3 is one setting away.

  • 04

    Play in your language

    Translation mod support is here. Spanish and French are the first additional languages: ZARF's Spanish mod in the catalogue, and the disc's own French as a setting.

  • 05

    Mod support

    Texture and language mods have a curated catalogue in the app. Your own music plays in the Beatbox, menus and fights, with the cut song "Holler If Ya Hear Me" restored too.

  • 06

    Controllers & keyboard

    Xbox, DualSense, Switch Pro and more, with remapping, hot-plug and rumble. Keyboard and mouse can carry gameplay bindings too.

  • 07

    One app for everything

    Def Jam Recompiled sets up the game, then handles Play, settings, controls, mods, updates, repair and uninstall. There's nothing else to keep track of.

  • 08

    Cheat menu

    Infinite health, max Blazin', giant and tiny fighters, fighter swaps, Story cash and development points.

  • 09

    Add to Steam

    On Windows, Linux and Steam Deck, one button beside Play puts the game in your Steam library, artwork included.

Install

One app. Four steps to the first round.

  1. Have your copy

    Your own USA Xbox disc image (ISO/XISO) or extracted folder, matching the supported dump manifest.

  2. Download the app

    Pick your platform below. The app holds the port's source and tools, never the game.

  3. Follow first run

    Choose your copy, then Modern (widescreen at 2×, recommended) or Original (4:3, console look). The app checks your copy and builds the game locally, in several minutes.

  4. Play

    Hit Play. New releases show up in the app, and updating keeps your saves, settings and mods.

Mods

Make it yours.

The app's Mods page has two tabs. Browse is a curated, signed catalogue. Installed holds everything you have, with one shared load order where later packs win. Nothing downloads or switches on by itself, and an update can be rolled back to the previous version.

  • Upscaled Original Textures: generated on your computer from your own copy.
  • HD Widescreen Menus: every menu and background, redrawn for widescreen.
  • HD Fighters: higher-resolution fighter textures.
  • ZARF's Spanish Translation: text, subtitles, fonts and recreated HD images.
  • Your own music: MP3, FLAC, Ogg or WAV files, managed on the Songs page.
  • Your own packs: the getting-started guide takes you through your first one.

Mods today change textures and language; your own songs play through the Songs page. Custom fighters, stages and game modes are coming up on the roadmap.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Do I need the original game?

Yes. You need your own USA Xbox copy, as a disc image (ISO/XISO) or an extracted folder, matching the supported dump manifest. The app checks it before building. PS2 and GameCube copies can't be used.

Why does the app build the game instead of downloading it?

The project never distributes game files or a compiled game. The app carries the port's source and tools, translates the game code from your copy and compiles it on your computer. Your original copy is left untouched.

How does the port work?

The xboxrecomp toolchain translates the Xbox executable into C, compiled ahead of time. The game's code runs on an Xbox compatibility runtime, with graphics, audio and input supplied by your computer. It's a static recompilation, not an emulator.

Will it run on my computer?

Supported: Windows x64, Macs with Apple Silicon, x86-64 Linux and the Steam Deck. Performance depends on your hardware, settings and the scene. Intel Macs aren't supported, and running the Windows build under Proton is untested.

Does it work on Steam Deck?

Yes, natively. In Desktop Mode, download the AppImage and open it, follow first run, then choose Add to Steam. From then on the game starts from Gaming Mode with its library artwork.

Why does Windows or macOS warn me about the app?

The app isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen or macOS Gatekeeper may ask you to confirm. Each release publishes a SHA-256 checksum and provenance file so you can check the download.

Can I play online?

No. Up to four players can play locally with gamepads and a keyboard. Network play isn't supported.

I used the old setup. How do I move to the app?

Download the app and open it. First run finds an existing installation, or you can point it at one under Use an existing installation. Your saves, settings and mods are kept.

How do updates work? Will I lose my saves?

The app checks GitHub for new releases when it starts (you can turn this off) and updates itself. When your game is older than the app, Update game builds it again from your copy in about three to ten minutes. Saves, settings and mods are kept, and the previous version stays playable until the new build is ready. Repair and uninstall are under Help.

Is it finished?

All game functionality works, and the port keeps improving based on player feedback. Open bugs are in GitHub Issues, and each release's notes list its known limits.

Something's broken. Where do I report it?

Start with troubleshooting, then open a GitHub issue with your version, OS, CPU/GPU, controller and the relevant log lines. Please don't attach F9 bug bundles, game files or dumps.
